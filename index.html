Generational bridge

Family origins and cultural identity are connected in different ways. Parental birthplaces provide one perspective on reported ancestry, while participation in cultural activities offers another view of how people engage with cultural life across age groups.

THE STORY SO FAR

Australia’s multicultural landscape continues to evolve, but diversity is experienced differently across regions and generations. Multiculturalism is not just about who we are, but how we live and connect.
