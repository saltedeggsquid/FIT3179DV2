A NOTE ON PLACE

One country, many local stories.

Australia's diversity is not spread evenly across the map. Language communities and countries of birth reveal different patterns of concentration, making local geography essential to understanding the national picture.

GEOGRAPHY IS PART OF THE STORY

↳Language across the landscape: The proportion of residents speaking a language other than English at home varies substantially across SA2s. Some areas show much higher proportions than others, revealing that linguistic diversity is geographically uneven.

↳Where language communities gather: Communities other than English-speakers appear concentrated in metropolitan and coastal areas, particularly along the eastern and southeastern seaboard. The pattern varies by selected language, so use the dropdown to compare communities.

↳The biggest birthplaces: India and England rank among the largest overseas-born populations in 2025, followed by China. The chart shows that a relatively small group of countries accounts for substantial portions of the overseas-born population.
